## Recurring debates (plain English)

1. **Does AI make doctors worse at their own skills?** A 2025 study found
   doctors who'd been using AI during colonoscopy found fewer early growths
   once it was switched off (22% of exams vs 28%).
   https://www.statnews.com/2025/08/12/ai-deskilling-doctors-colonoscopy-study-lancet/
2. **Do AI scribes really fix burnout?** Surveys show big drops (one system:
   53% burned out before, 31% after), but measured time savings are modest,
   around 8–10%. Life-changing, or a morale boost? And will hospitals just
   book more patients into the time saved?
   https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/physician-health/how-much-can-ambient-ai-scribes-help-cut-doctor-burnout
3. **Patients arrive with "Dr. ChatGPT".** ChatGPT Health (2026) lets people
   connect their own records and ask about them. Privacy, trust and skipped
   visits vs better-informed patients.
   https://www.healthaffairs.org/content/forefront/chatgpt-health-becomes-health-record-direct-consumer-care
4. **Doctors quietly use AI answer tools. Can they trust them?** OpenEvidence
   is widely used; concerns are wrong answers, no independent quality checks,
   no rule to tell patients, and drug-company advertising funding.
   https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/openevidence-ai-doctor-medical-physician-login-app-what-npi-uptodate-rcna341064
5. **Evidence first, or adopt now?** Topol, Kohane and Harvey: test AI like new
   drugs. Wachter: it only has to beat today's overloaded system. Plus: when
   AI is wrong, who's responsible?
   https://substack.com/@erictopol/note/c-253064463
6. **AI chatbots as therapists.** Psychiatrists warn they lack real empathy and
   can be unsafe, especially for young people.
   https://iapp.org/news/a/us-house-subcommittee-seeks-answers-to-mental-health-issues-stemming-from-AI-chatbot-use
