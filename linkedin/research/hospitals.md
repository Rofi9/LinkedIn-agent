# Hospitals implementing AI, 2025–2026

Researched 2026-09-28 via web search. Several publisher sites blocked page
fetches (MedCity, WEF, Healthcare IT News, Frontiers, digitalhealth.net), so
some items rest on search summaries. **Open each link before quoting a
number.** Items marked (verify) were not fully confirmed.

## United States

**1. Cleveland Clinic** · announced 19 Feb 2025; results Jun–Aug 2026
- Rolled out Ambience, an ambient AI scribe: it listens to the visit and
  drafts the note, and the doctor reviews and signs it. Enterprise go-live
  10 Mar 2025; 4,000+ outpatient clinicians onboarded in 4 months.
- A year on: 97% clinician satisfaction, 70% use among established users,
  60% of users said it made them more likely to stay in practice.
- Lesson: framed as "a gift, not a mandate". Doctors weren't asked to see
  more patients in exchange.
- https://newsroom.clevelandclinic.org/2025/02/19/cleveland-clinic-announces-the-rollout-of-ambience-healthcares-ai-platform
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s44401-026-00144-6
- https://medcitynews.com/2026/08/cleveland-clinic-ai-healthcare-ambient-scribes/

**2. Kaiser Permanente** · 2025 (NEJM Catalyst)
- AI scribe across 8 regions, 600 medical offices and 40 hospitals, after a
  10-week pilot.
- 7,260 physicians, 2.5M+ visits over 14 months, an estimated 15,791
  documentation hours saved. Published how quality-assurance checks shaped
  the rollout.
- https://catalyst.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/CAT.25.0040
- https://divisionofresearch.kaiserpermanente.org/quality-assurance-informs-large-scale-use-of-ambient-ai-clinical-documentation/

**3. Stanford Health Care** · June 2025
- ChatEHR: clinicians "chat" with a patient's record to ask questions and get
  summaries.
- Started with 33 users, planned ~150 by Aug 2025; tested against Stanford's
  MedHELM evaluation framework before wider rollout.
- https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2025/06/ai-medical-records-chat-ehr

**4. Memorial Hermann Health System** · Sept 2026
- Epic's "Art" AI drafts plain-language explanations of test results for
  patients; a clinician edits each before sending.
- Saves only ~9 seconds per note, but patients sent 32% fewer follow-up
  questions; 55,000+ messages drafted.
- Lesson: "seconds saved" is the wrong metric for this kind of tool.
- https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/memorial-hermann-health-system-clinicians-use-epics-ai-tool-to-help-patients-better-understand-test-results-302870772.html
- https://healthsystemcio.com/2026/09/10/in-basket-ai-wrong-metric/

**5. Sentara Health** · launched late 2024; reported 2025–26 (verify date)
- Organization-wide "Data and AI Literacy" program: 60,000+ modules completed,
  monthly workshops, team sessions, a two-hour "AI Trailblazer
  Certification".
- https://www.healthcareitnews.com/news/how-sentara-health-built-enterprise-ai-literacy-program
- https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/how-health-systems-are-tackling-ais-workforce-readiness-gap/

**6. Sharp HealthCare** · lawsuit filed 26 Nov 2025 (cautionary)
- Proposed class action alleges visits were recorded with the Abridge AI
  scribe without valid consent, and that the tool inserted false "patient
  consented" statements into charts. **Allegations only; nothing proven.**
- https://www.kpbs.org/news/health/2025/12/11/lawsuit-claims-sharp-healthcare-secretly-recorded-exam-room-conversations-without-patient-consent
- https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/legal-regulatory-issues/patient-sues-sharp-healthcare-over-ambient-ai-use/

## Canada

**7. Ontario Auditor General** · May 2026 (cautionary)
- In a provincial procurement test of 20 AI scribe products, 9 invented
  clinical details (referrals or tests that never happened) and 12 recorded
  the wrong drug in at least one test.
- Recommended system controls that make doctors confirm they reviewed each
  note.
- https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/ai-scribe-system-hallucinations-9.7197049

## UK / Europe

**8. Great Ormond Street Hospital and London partners** · 2025; AI strategy 2025–2028
- Led a trial of the TORTUS AI scribe across 9 London NHS sites, then began
  rolling it out in its own outpatient clinics.
- 17,000+ encounters; clinicians spent 23.5% more time interacting with
  patients and reported 35% less feeling of being overwhelmed. At St George's
  emergency department, time to write the first note halved.
- https://www.gosh.nhs.uk/news/researchgosh-led-trial-of-ai-scribe-technology-shows-transformative-benefits-for-patients-and-clinicians-across-london/
- https://www.gosh.nhs.uk/about-us/our-strategy/our-ai-strategy-for-2025-2028/ambient-voice-technology-ai-scribe-faqs/

**9. NHS England** · Apr 2025 – Jul 2026
- National guidance on AI scribes and a supplier registry; MHRA clarified how
  they're regulated. In mid-2025 the BMA advised GPs to pause AI scribes until
  data-protection and safety checks were done.
- July 2026: NHS backed a wider rollout as part of £10bn technology funding
  (verify).
- https://www.england.nhs.uk/long-read/guidance-on-the-use-of-ai-enabled-ambient-scribing-products-in-health-and-care-settings/
- https://www.digitalhealth.net/2026/07/nhs-backs-rollout-of-integrated-avt-as-part-of-10bn-tech-funding/

**10. Quirónsalud (Spain)** · study published July 2026
- AI scribe across outpatient clinics: 2.33M uses Sept 2024 – Dec 2025; use
  grew from 2.7% to ~31% of visits.
- Documentation improved; consultations didn't get shorter.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13381512/

## In plain English

- **AI scribes are mainstream.** They listen to the visit and draft the note.
  They're the most common first use of generative AI in hospitals worldwide.
- **Big systems pilot, then scale.** How a tool is introduced matters as much
  as the tool.
- **The AI drafts; the human is responsible.** Ontario's scribes invented
  tests and recorded wrong drugs, which is why doctors must read every note.
- **Consent and privacy are a real legal risk** (the Sharp allegations).
- **Time saved isn't the only measure.** Memorial Hermann: 9 seconds per note,
  32% fewer follow-up questions.
- **Training is becoming formal**: Sentara's certification, India's national
  course.
- **Gulf and Asian systems move fast; Armenia and the CIS are early.**
