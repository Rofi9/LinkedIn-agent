Bringing in LinkedIn posts with Claude in Chrome
  1. Open a LinkedIn post, or a doctor's profile activity, in Chrome.
  2. Ask the Claude extension: "Copy the full text of this post, the author's name and role, and the post's link."
  3. Paste the result below and press Analyze. Do this one post at a time, at your normal reading pace. Bulk collection breaks LinkedIn's terms and can get the account restricted.
